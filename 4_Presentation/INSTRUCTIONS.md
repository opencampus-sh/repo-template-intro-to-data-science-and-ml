# Project Presentation

## Overview

The project presentation is your opportunity to showcase your entire machine learning project, from problem formulation to your final results. This presentation should provide a comprehensive overview, explain the decisions you've made along the way, and highlight the results and findings of your project.

**Maximum Duration**: The presentation must not exceed the time announced by the course instructor (usually 8–10 minutes). It is followed by a short Q&A part.

## Guidelines

1.  **Introduction**: Briefly introduce your team and which team member was responsible for which part of the project.

2.  **Dataset Characteristics**: Provide a quick overview of the new variables you created. Show at least two bar charts with confidence intervals for newly created variables.

3.  **Baseline Model**: Quickly recap your baseline model, the selected feature variables and its performance.

4.  **Model Definition and Evaluation**: Discuss the models you've implemented, the feature engineering steps you've taken, and how you evaluated their performance. Include a screenshot of the code you used to implement the model.

5.  **Results**: Report the MAPE for each product category.

6.  **Challenges and Errors**: Discuss the most challenging issue or error you encountered during the data preparation or modeling phase, and how you overcame it.

7.  **Q&A**: Be prepared to answer questions from the audience or instructor.

## Structure

Your presentation should generally follow this structure:

1.  **Title Slide**: Project name, group number, names of the project team, date, and any other relevant information.

2.  **Dataset Characteristics**: 2-3 slides.

3.  **Baseline Model**: 1 slide.

4.  **Model Definition and Evaluation**: 2-3 slides.

5.  **Results**: 1 slide.

6.  **Challenges and Errors**: 1 slide.

7.  **Q&A**: Final slide indicating the Q&A part.

## Submission Instructions

The project is submitted on [EduHub](https://edu.opencampus.sh) as a team project. Only **one** team member submits on behalf of the team, but all team members must be confirmed authors of the project on EduHub beforehand.

### 1. Prepare the repository

1.  **Slides**: Save your presentation slides in `4_Presentation` as a PDF or PowerPoint file and update the link in this folder's [README](README.md).
2.  **README**: Complete the main [README](../README.md) with your results and the description of your data, and check that every folder README describes your work.
3.  **Cover image**: Replace `CoverImage/cover_image.png` with a key visualization of your project.
4.  **Public repository**: Set your GitHub repository to public. Make sure it does not contain passwords, API keys or personal data.

### 2. Submit on EduHub

1.  Log in at [edu.opencampus.sh](https://edu.opencampus.sh) and open your team project of the course.
2.  Fill in the project details and upload the four deliverables:
    -   **Documentation** (PDF, DOCX, ODT or ZIP, max. 23 MB): the project report, e.g. the main README exported as PDF. Follow the documentation instructions that you can download from your project on EduHub.
    -   **Presentation** (PDF, PPT/PPTX or ODP, max. 23 MB): the slides of your final presentation.
    -   **External link**: the link to your public GitHub repository.
    -   **Cover image**: a key visualization of your project, shown on the project tile.
3.  When the submission checklist is complete, one team member clicks **"Submit for review"**. The project is then read-only for the team.

Documentation and presentation are publicly downloadable after the upload.

**Deadline**: All submissions are reviewed after the deadline. The instructor either approves the project or sends it back for revision; certificates are issued for approved projects.
